DP Sheet

The DP Sheet is the single most powerful performance-enhancing system I have implemented into my daily life. It's the first thing I do in the morning before starting work, and the last thing I do at night before turning off my laptop. Total daily time: 5 minutes.

Here's the psychology behind why I believe it's so powerful, and why I think every high-performer should implement it too:

  • The only way to get consistent output is to have consistent input, and the best way to keep input consistent is to measure it.
  • Making informed decisions requires data. So when things go badly (or well), unless you can see what happened through data, you'll make an uninformed decision and are likely to go in the wrong direction—or at least not in the most optimal one.
  • Humans can achieve the highest levels of performance, with the most optimal effort-to-output ratio, by building the right habits and getting rid of bad ones as early in life as possible.
  • The first step to both eliminating a bad habit and forming a new one is to be constantly aware of that habit and of your progress towards eliminating or forming it.
  • Without specific KPIs and deadlines, eliminating or forming a habit remains a wishy-washy dream. The moment there's a measurable and visual way to track progress, it becomes a goal, and the chances of achieving it increase exponentially.

I thank Sam Ovens for inspiring me to create this, and I hope that seeing my DP Sheet can inspire you and have a positive impact on your life.
